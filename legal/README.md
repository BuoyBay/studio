# Studio contribution agreements

Original outside contributions to Studio's AGPL application core require the
applicable Harmony agreement below. Contributions confined to permissive shared
modules, tools or generated-project scaffolds do not require a CLA or DCO.

- [Individual CLA](cla-individual.md): work owned by an individual contributor.
- [Entity CLA](cla-entity.md): work owned by an employer or another entity.
- [Agreement overview](cla.md): scope and retained community licensing.
- [Signing](SIGNING.md): current maintainer-reviewed process.
- [License boundaries](../LICENSING.md): AGPL core, permissive components and scaffolds.

The Harmony templates use Option Five and retain their attribution and terms.

as Project Steward. Copyright and third-party rights remain as stated in the
agreements and retained upstream notices.
