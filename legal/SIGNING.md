# Signing the Studio CLA

A CLA is required before merging original outside work into Studio's AGPL core.
It is not required for changes confined to permissive shared modules, developer
tools or generated-project scaffolds. A mixed patch that also adds original
AGPL-core work requires the applicable agreement for that work.

## Choose the agreement

Read the [individual CLA](cla-individual.md) if you own the work. If your employer
or another entity owns it, an authorized representative must sign the
[entity CLA](cla-entity.md); an individual signature cannot grant rights owned
by that entity. Identify third-party material and retain its upstream terms;
do not present it as original work covered by your own grant.

## Current signing process

Contact a maintainer on the contribution's pull request to arrange signing and
record the agreement version and covered contribution. The Project Steward is
Donald Lee Lunder 3rd, doing business as Zerova (GitHub [donl](https://github.com/donl)).
The maintainer verifies the contributor or entity signatory and records acceptance
before merging AGPL-core work. Keep private contact and signature documents out
of public commits unless their publication is separately agreed.

GitHub CLA automation is not currently active. Do not assume that posting a bot
command or adding a JSON row completes review. If automation is enabled later,
maintainers must first document and validate its signing and exemption behavior.

Contributors retain copyright. The additional-licensing grant and community
license covenant are described in the agreements and [LICENSING.md](../LICENSING.md).
The agreements do not grant compiler/runtime, third-party or trademark rights.
